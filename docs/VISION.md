**Game Title:** Delta Wrecker

**One-line Pitch:** Become an independent orbital wrecker — keeping space clean and satellites alive while making a profit.

**Core Fantasy:** You run a small one-ship salvage company. When satellites fail or debris threatens active orbits, you’re the one who answers the call. You track them down, grapple on, and either deliver them to the junkyard or tow them in for repairs.

**Core Gameplay:**
- Use real orbital mechanics to intercept targets and establish perch orbits
- Execute precise burns
- Play a fun, arcade-style grappling minigame when you get close
- Carefully tow your payload while managing thrust limits and collision risks

**Progression:** The game is divided into three distinct phases that naturally teach orbital mechanics:
- Phase 1: Near-circular orbits (simple debris hauling)
- Phase 2: Eccentric orbits + COLA (salvage runs)
- Phase 3: Inclined orbits + advanced towing

**Goal:** Build something technically impressive in Rust, deepen my understanding of orbital mechanics, and create a game I’m proud to show others.
